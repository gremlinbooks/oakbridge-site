How It Works

From first call to friction-free in 30 days.

1

Discovery Call (30 min, free)

We talk through your operation, your biggest pain points, and what's eating your time. No pitch. Just a real conversation about where you're bleeding.

2

Operational Audit

You fill a one-page intake. We do a deep audit call (45 min), map your friction points, and deliver a written report within 48 hours — scorecard, friction map, quick wins, automation opportunities, and a 90-day roadmap.

We pick 1-2 low-risk fixes and implement them immediately. You see results in the first two weeks — not in six months.

Systematic Optimization (90 days+)

We work the roadmap — process redesign, automation, dashboards. Ongoing monthly engagement with regular check-ins and measurable progress.
